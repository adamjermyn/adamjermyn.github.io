Privacy policy for my apps

This covers the personal iOS and macOS apps I build for my own use, including Ledger (bundle ID com.adamjermyn.Ledger). These apps are not offered to the public; the only user is me.

What data the apps use. Ledger reads my own financial data: account balances and transactions, including Apple Card data read on-device through Apple’s FinanceKit. It uses this only to show me my spending, categories and cash-flow plans.

Where data goes. The data stays on my own devices and in my own private storage. It is not sent to any third party, not sold, not used for advertising or tracking, and not combined with anyone else’s data.

Access is read-only. The apps never initiate payments, transfers or any change at a bank or at Apple.

Removing data. Revoking the app’s access in iOS Settings stops it reading new data, and deleting the app removes what it stored on the device.

Contact. Questions about this policy: adamjermyn@gmail.com.
